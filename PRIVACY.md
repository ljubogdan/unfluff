# Privacy

Unfluff collects nothing.

- **No network requests.** The extension never contacts LinkedIn, the author, or anyone else. There is no server, no analytics, and no third-party code.
- **Settings** (your filters, muted words and authors) are stored with `chrome.storage.sync`, which your browser may sync between your own devices through your browser account.
- **The noise report** is a set of daily counts (posts seen, posts per noise type) stored locally with `chrome.storage.local` and kept for 30 days. It contains no post content and no names. "Reset report" in the popup deletes it.
- **Share cards** are drawn in your browser and only leave it if you post them yourself.

Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.
